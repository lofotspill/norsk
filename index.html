Your first day at a Norwegian school

You have just arrived in Norway. You do not speak Norwegian yet, and that is okay. Walk around, meet people, and learn the words you need to make friends.

Write your name first.

🔊 Sound: this game reads Norwegian aloud with your device's voice. If you hear nothing or an English voice, install a Norwegian voice. On a Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices → Norwegian.
